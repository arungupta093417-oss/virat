Varushka ❤️ — Two Hearts, One Beautiful Love Story ✨

Virat Kohli and Anushka Sharma, lovingly known as Varushka, met in 2013 during an advertisement shoot. Their beautiful love story grew stronger over the years. They got married on 11 December 2017 in Italy in a private ceremony. They welcomed their daughter Vamika in 2021 and son Akaay in 2024. Their journey shows that true love grows with trust, respect, and togetherness. ❤️✨
